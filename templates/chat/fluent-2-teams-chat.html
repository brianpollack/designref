Anatomy

Received messageA 32px avatar, then a stack: a byline (name and time, 12px, second-level gray) over a bubble. The bubble is 4px-cornered on the neutral background 3 fill with padding of 8px top, 16px right and left, 16px bottom, and is at most the row width less 100px.
Attached messageA follow-up from the same sender: no avatar and no byline, 2px above instead of 16px, shifted 40px so its bubble lines up under the first. The byline stays in the DOM for screen readers.
My messagePushed to the right with at least 50px clear on the left, filled with the brand background 2 tint, and with no avatar or byline. A 16px status icon sits at its lower right.
Status iconSending: circle. Received: check in a circle. Read: filled eye. Failed: filled warning. Blocked: filled flag. Scheduled: clock. All 16px; brand colored, red when failed or blocked.
DividerA Fluent Divider with text: lines either side of a 12px label, 12px of space around it, line in stroke 2.
Reaction pill26px high, 13px radius, 1px stroke 2, minimum 41px wide, an emoji and a 12px count. The row has no height of its own, so the pills hang over the bubble’s lower edge; the bubble gets extra bottom margin to make room. The overlap offsets are ours: react-chat supplies the zero-height container and nothing more.
Hover toolbarFive quick reactions, reply and more, in a shadow 4 card at the bubble’s top right. The react-chat package provides only the slot, so its geometry is ours.
ComposeA Fluent Textarea in the outline appearance, at least 52px tall, with a 1px stroke 1 outline and a stronger bottom edge. Focus draws a 2px brand bar along the bottom. Format, emoji and attach sit in the box; send is brand colored when enabled.

Specs

Token names are Fluent 2’s own. Values are resolved from the Teams theme: the Teams dark neutrals and the brandTeams ramp, by step index (the hex comments beside brand tokens in lightColor.ts and darkColor.ts show a placeholder ramp, so they are not used).

Behavior

GroupingThe consumer marks a message attached when the previous one is from the same sender; the component only styles it. Teams does not publish the time window.
ReactionsHover a message and choose one. Teams shows up to five quick emoji and a More reactions button; the reaction appears just below the message; tapping it again removes yours; a message holds up to 20 reactions. Which five are the defaults is not documented; we use the first five of the six named types: like, heart, laugh, surprised, sad. Angry is the sixth.
IconsRegular at rest, Filled while hovered, as Fluent does for toolbar and selected states. Both are in the DOM and CSS swaps them, so there is no flash.
FocusThe message body is focusable and traps focus in its group, so Tab reaches its toolbar and links; the component sets tabindex="0" and a limited focus trap. Here, Tab on a message reaches the toolbar because focus within shows it.
HighlightA message that is jumped to flashes a marigold background and a 0.1rem dark-orange inset ring that fade over 4 seconds.
MentionsType @ and pick from the menu. @channel and @team work in channels; @everyone works in a chat if the owner enabled it. The recipient gets an Activity notification that opens at that message.
UnreadUnread chats are bold with a count; muted chats are bold with no count. There is no dot.
Edit and deleteHover your own message for Edit, then Done. Delete is in More options and offers Undo.

Accessibility

Key (Teams, Windows)Does
Ctrl + RGo to the compose box.
Ctrl + MMove focus to the message pane.
Alt + Shift + RReply to the last message.
Ctrl + Alt + RReact to the last message.
Ctrl + JJump to the last read or newest message.
Shift + EnterNew line; Ctrl + Enter sends once the box is expanded.
  • This demo wires Enter, Shift+Enter and Tab only; the Teams shortcuts above are what a production build should add.
  • Status icons carry a text label, because Fluent’s icons are not read as meaning on their own. Fluent’s own guidance for avatars says presence icons are hard to see at 32px and below, so pair them with text: the header’s “Available” does that.
  • Each message has a summary name (sender, time and status) so focusing it reads the whole message.
  • Focus ring is a 2px line in the foreground color, inside the element, so it is visible on every fill in both themes.

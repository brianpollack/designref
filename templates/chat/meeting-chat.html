Anatomy

PanelA side panel beside the call, titled “In-call messages”. In Meet it opens from the Chat button at the bottom right of the call.
Host controlA switch, shown to hosts only, that turns sending off for everyone. In Meet it is “Let contributors send messages”.
Persistence noteOne line saying who can see messages and when they disappear. People do not expect a call’s chat to vanish; say it.
Pinned messageA banner above the list that shows the pinned message and jumps to it. Pin appears on hover, as in Meet.
MessageDense: a name and time on one line, then the text. No avatars, because the call already shows faces. Messages from the same person close together share a name.
ComposerA single-line pill and a send button. No formatting row, no attachments: the call is not the place for either.
Chat offThe composer is replaced by a sentence naming the cause. People can still read.

Specs

Google publishes no pixel values for Meet’s panel, so sizes here are our own. What is taken from Meet’s help page is behavior: who sees messages, persistence, the host switch, and pinning.

Behavior

Who sees itEveryone in the call. With continuous meeting chat, internal guests on the calendar invite also see it; external guests and anyone not invited see only what is sent while they are present.
PersistenceBy default messages sent before you join do not appear, and all messages disappear when you leave. With continuous chat, internal invitees keep the history. Pinned messages are the exception that people can find again.
Host offHosts can turn off “Let contributors send messages”, which needs host management on. Participants can still read. For one-time or instant meetings the setting resets when the meeting ends.
PinComputer users hover a message and pin it. You can pin and unpin your own; hosts can unpin anyone’s. Meet does not show pinned messages on mobile.
MobileOn a phone the panel is a sheet from the bottom. Internal guests on mobile are sent to the Google Chat or Gmail app for calendar-linked chat.

Accessibility

  • The panel is an aside with a name, and the message list is role="log": new messages are announced politely, so they do not talk over the people in the call.
  • The host control is a role="switch" with aria-checked and a visible label.
  • Pin is a toggle button (aria-pressed) that is reachable from the keyboard: it shows with focus inside the row, not only on hover.
  • Chat off is stated in words. A disabled input with no explanation leaves a screen-reader user guessing.

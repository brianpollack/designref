Anatomy

CardThe pressed item: a link that opens the photo. Not draggable, no text selection or touch callout, so the hold belongs to the menu.
BackdropThe modal dialog’s ::backdrop: the whole screen dimmed and blurred. Only the preview and the list stay sharp.
PreviewA copy of the card’s image laid exactly over the card, then scaled up with rounded corners and a shadow. The card underneath hides until the preview lands again.
Action listThe dialog’s own box: a 250px material surface with its own blur and 14px corners, 10px from the preview.
Quick actionsThe top row: the three most common actions as icon-over-label buttons (UIKit’s medium element size).
RowLabel first, icon trailing, as iOS draws them. 44px tall, 0.5px hairline between rows.
Group bandAn 8px band between groups. Three groups at most.
Destructive itemLast, in its own group, red label and icon.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onA 500ms press that moves less than 8px; right-click; Shift+F10 or the Menu key on a focused card; Android’s own long-press (it fires contextmenu). A 10ms haptic tick where navigator.vibrate exists.
While holdingThe card shrinks to 95% over the whole hold, so the press reads as progress. Moving more than 8px (a scroll) or lifting early cancels, and it springs back in 200ms.
LiftThe preview picks up at 95% and springs to 104% over 420ms while its corners round from 0 to 14px and its shadow grows. The backdrop dims and blurs to 14px at the same time.
List placementBelow the card with left edges aligned. Right-aligned when it would cross the right edge; above when there is no room below (position-try-fallbacks). It grows out of the corner nearest the card.
Drag to pickAfter a hold, keep the finger down, slide onto an action and lift: it runs. The row under the finger is highlighted. Lifting over the preview leaves the menu open; lifting anywhere else closes it (light dismiss).
ActionsRun and close. Favorite toggles the heart on the photo. Delete is shown, not wired; a real app asks to confirm.
DismissA tap outside the list (light dismiss), Esc, or a tap on the preview (an app would open the photo). Everything reverses in 220ms and the card reappears as the preview lands.

Accessibility

Shift+F10 MenuOn a focused card: lift it and focus the first action.
↓ → / ↑ ←Next or previous action in reading order; the top row reads left to right (native focusgroup). After a touch or pointer open, ↓ enters at the top and ↑ at the bottom.
Home EndFirst and last action.
Enter SpaceRun the focused action and close.
EscClose. Focus returns to the card.
  • showModal() makes the page inert and moves focus into the dialog: to the list, its first focusable element. No autofocus attribute: it would also queue a page-level autofocus that can scroll an embedding page. Esc and light dismiss are native through closedby="any". Focus returns to the card because script focuses it just before opening.
  • The dialog is named after the photo (its aria-label is copied on open). The preview is a decorative copy, aria-hidden.
  • focusgroup="menu" infers menu and menuitem roles. Favorite is a menuitemcheckbox; the top row is a named group; group bands are <hr> separators. inline block is added because menu alone answers only ↑/↓.
  • A long-press can’t be discovered and isn’t open to keyboard or switch users on its own: the same menu opens with right-click and Shift+F10, and every action should also live somewhere visible, like the photo viewer’s toolbar.
  • Not built here: reversing the list order when it opens above (iOS does, so the nearest item is the most common); it would make the visual and keyboard orders disagree.

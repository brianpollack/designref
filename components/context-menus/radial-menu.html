Anatomy

Hit layerA transparent popover over the whole surface. It captures the pointer, so a direction counts anywhere on screen, and it keeps clicks off the canvas while the ring is open.
PlateA ring-shaped surface behind the items with a hairline on both edges. The hole between plate and hub lets the artwork show through.
Item44px round target, icon only. Item i of n sits at angle (i − 1) × 360° / n, clockwise from 12 o’clock, at x = sin(a)·r, y = −cos(a)·r. Eight items at most: the four compass points and the diagonals stay easy to hit by feel.
SectorThe 45° wedge of the plate that owns the highlighted item, inset 4px from the plate edges. It shows that picking is by direction, not by hitting the circle.
HubThe dead zone, drawn. Shows the highlighted tool’s name, or the current tool’s name dimmed while nothing is highlighted. An arc on its edge points at the pointer.
Current markerA 4px dot under the current tool’s icon.
Number hintThe item’s digit at its top-right, shown only while the keyboard is in use.
Layout ruleOpposite directions hold opposite tools: Brush up, Eraser down; Ellipse right, Pan left. Keep each tool in the same direction forever so the flick becomes muscle memory.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onRight button down; a 300ms still hold with the primary button, pen or touch (moving more than 6px first makes it a drag); Control-click on macOS; Shift+F10 or the Menu key on the focused canvas.
PlacementCentered on the pointer. Near an edge the center moves inward (clamp() in CSS) so the plate stays 12px inside the surface. Keyboard opens center on the canvas.
HighlightThe item whose 45° sector contains the pointer’s angle from the center, at any distance, even far past the ring. Inside the hub nothing is highlighted.
Press, drag, releaseWith the button still down, move more than 6px toward a tool and let go: it is picked. Let go inside the hub: nothing changes.
ClickLet go without moving (a plain right-click or hold): the ring stays open. Then click in any direction to pick, or in the hub to cancel.
MotionPlate and hub grow from 60%. Items fly out from the center along their radius, 240ms with a slight overshoot, each starting 15ms after the one before it, clockwise. Closing takes 110ms, all at once. No motion with reduced-motion set.
DismissEsc, a release or click in the hub, Tab, or focus leaving the window. The tool stays as it was.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
Shift+F10 MenuOn the focused canvas: open the ring with focus on the current tool.
→ ↓Next tool clockwise. Wraps from Select to Brush (native focusgroup).
← ↑Previous tool, counter-clockwise.
1–8Pick a tool by position, clockwise from 12 o’clock.
Enter SpacePick the focused tool and close.
EscClose without changing the tool. Focus returns to the canvas (native popover behavior).
TabClose and move on. Focus is never trapped.
  • Roles: menu (inferred by focusgroup="menu") with menuitemradio items and aria-checked on the current tool. A menu, because it is transient and closes on a pick; radio items, because exactly one tool is active. A radiogroup was rejected: its arrows change the value at once, which would switch tools on every key press.
  • focusgroup="menu inline block wrap": menu alone answers only ↑/↓ in Chrome; naming both axes adds ←/→.
  • Each tool’s name is real text inside its button, so it is the accessible name. CSS only draws it in the hub. Digits are exposed as aria-keyshortcuts; the visible hint is aria-hidden.
  • The canvas is role="application" with a label and a description that names the shortcut. Plate, sector, arc and dot are decorative.
  • Highlight comes from a data-highlight attribute, not :focus, so the pointer’s highlight shows even when the window does not have focus. Keyboard focus adds a 2px ring.
  • Not built here: Blender’s hotkey model (hold a key, flick, release the key to pick) and nested rings. Both reuse open() and pick().

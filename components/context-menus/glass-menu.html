Anatomy

PanelA translucent tint over a blurred, saturated copy of whatever is behind it. Squircle corners, 6px inner padding. Values are our own, inspired by Apple’s Liquid Glass menus in macOS and iOS 26; they are not Apple’s specs.
Lit edgeThree hairlines in one shadow token: a bright 1px line on the top edge, a faint 1px rim all round, and a 0.5px dark outline that separates the panel from light content.
SheenA white gloss that fades out over the top 64px, so the panel reads as lit from above.
ItemCompact 24px rows: 16px icon slot, label, then a shortcut or chevron at the end. The slot is kept even when empty so labels align.
HighlightAccent fill with white label, icon and shortcut. Corners 14px, concentric with the 20px panel.
Checkable itemCheck mark in the icon slot when on, empty slot when off.
Submenu chevronShown on Share as a visual only; this page builds no submenu. See Cascading Submenus.
Disabled itemDimmed and skipped by arrow keys.
Destructive itemLast, after a separator. Red label and icon; red fill when highlighted.
Separator1px, inset to the item padding, between groups of related commands.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onRight-click, Control-click on macOS, long-press on touch, Shift+F10 or the Menu key on a focused photo.
PlacementTop-left corner at the pointer; flips to the other side of the pointer on whichever axis runs out of room.
MotionFades in and grows from 96% out of the corner nearest the pointer, 140ms. Fades out in 180ms. No motion with reduced motion set.
Contrast on glassThe tint is 80% opaque in light and 84% in dark, and the shortcut and destructive colors are darker (light) or lighter (dark) than usual. Together they keep every label, shortcut and destructive label at 4.5:1 or more over the darkest (light theme) and brightest (dark theme) spot of this photo wall, measured through the blur. At 74% the dark shortcuts fell to 3.1:1. Lower the opacity only if your content is known to be calmer.
Reduce transparencyWith the system setting on, the panel switches to an opaque fill and drops the blur (prefers-reduced-transparency).
PointerThe highlight follows the pointer and takes keyboard focus with it, so arrow keys continue from there. A click runs the command and closes the menu.
Checkable itemToggles, stays open for 250ms so the new state is visible, then closes.
Not builtmacOS flashes the chosen item once before closing, and Liquid Glass refracts the content at its edges. Both are left out to keep the code small.
DismissEsc, a click or right-click elsewhere (which reopens at the new spot), Tab, or focus leaving the window.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
Shift+F10Open the menu for the focused photo, with focus on the first item.
↓ ↑Move between enabled items (native focusgroup). From a pointer open, the first press enters the menu.
Home EndFirst and last item.
Enter SpaceRun the command, or toggle Favorite.
EscClose and return focus to the photo.
TabClose the menu and move on.
  • focusgroup="menu" infers role="menu" and menuitem. Favorite sets menuitemcheckbox and aria-checked. Separators are <hr>.
  • Contrast is measured on the glass, not on a plain background (see Behavior). Worst case, light / dark: labels 10.3:1 / 7.8:1, shortcuts 5.1:1 / 4.8:1, destructive labels 4.8:1 / 4.6:1.
  • The highlight fill is darker than system blue (#007AFF gives white text only 4.0:1); white on it is 5.4:1 light and 6.4:1 dark.
  • Keyboard focus and hover share one highlight. On dark glass the blue fill is close to the glass in lightness, so the white label carries the change; as on macOS, the item is marked by hue and by its label inverting.
  • Shortcut text is aria-hidden; add aria-keyshortcuts where the shortcuts really work. When Share gets a real submenu, add aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.

Anatomy

MenuOne square surface on $layer with a 0 2px 6px shadow. 4px padding top and bottom, none at the sides, so item fills run edge to edge. 192px minimum width with icons (160px without), 288px maximum.
Item32px tall at the default size, 16px side padding. Label in body-compact-01 and $text-secondary; long labels truncate with an ellipsis.
Sizesxs 24px, sm 32px (default), md 40px, lg 48px. Match the size of the control that opens the menu. Only the row height changes.
ColumnsCheck (16px), icon (16px), label, shortcut, 8px apart. A menu with any icon gives every row the icon column; with any selectable item, every row gets the check column.
ShortcutRight-aligned text in the label’s style and color. Hidden from screen readers here; the action is announced by its label.
Selectable itemA 16px check mark in the first column when checked. Carbon also groups radio items under a label (MenuItemRadioGroup).
Divider1px $border-subtle across the full width, 4px above and below.
Disabled item$text-disabled (25% of the text color). No hover fill; arrow keys skip it.
Danger itemLooks like any item at rest. On hover and focus it fills with Red 60 and the label turns white. Put it last, after a divider.
v12 (alpha)Rounds the menu to 8px with 4px items and 4px side padding inside the surface. Stable v11, shown here, stays square.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onRight-click on a row, or Shift+F10 / the Menu key on a focused row. Carbon’s useContextMenu listens for contextmenu the same way.
PlacementTop-left corner at the pointer. Near the right or bottom edge it flips to end at the pointer instead, as Carbon’s own positioning does.
MotionNone for the menu: it appears and disappears at once. Hover fills fade in over 70ms on the productive standard curve.
Focus on openThe first enabled item takes focus every time, pointer or keyboard. Because Carbon styles :focus rather than :focus-visible, its outline shows after a right-click too.
HoverFills the item without moving focus, so a hovered item and the focused item can differ.
ChoosingEvery item closes the menu, including selectable ones (the check toggles first).
DismissEsc, Tab, a click outside, or choosing an item. Focus returns to the row.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
Shift+F10 or Menu keyOpen the menu for the focused row, focus on the first item.
↓ ↑Move between enabled items, wrapping at the ends.
Home EndFirst and last item (native focusgroup; Carbon has no equivalent).
Enter SpaceRun the action, or toggle Pin to top, and close.
Esc TabClose and return to the row.
  • focusgroup="menu" infers role="menu" and menuitem, as Carbon’s <ul role="menu"> and <li role="menuitem"> do. Pin to top is a menuitemcheckbox.
  • The danger item points at a description with aria-describedby, the job of Carbon’s dangerDescription prop, so color is not the only warning.
  • Focus outline: 2px $focus, Blue 60 on White and white on Gray 100. Under prefers-contrast it turns dotted, as in Carbon.
  • Contrast: $text-secondary on the menu is 7.1:1 light and 8.9:1 dark; white on the Red 60 danger fill is 5.0:1. Disabled text is intentionally low.
  • Rows need tabindex="0" for the keyboard path; in a real Carbon data table, the row or a cell carries focus.

Anatomy

TriggerA 28px square ⋯ button at the end of the row. Always visible, since touch has no hover. Muted at rest, tinted on hover, filled while its menu is open. Its accessible name says which row it acts on.
ContainerPopover surface with a 1px hairline border and a two-layer shadow, 4px from the trigger. Vertical padding only, so highlights run edge to edge.
ItemIcon and label, no shortcuts: this menu is tapped as often as it is clicked. 34px rows, 44px on touch screens.
Disabled itemDimmed, skipped by arrow keys, kept in place. Here a paid invoice can’t get a reminder and a draft has no PDF yet.
SeparatorFull-width 1px rule that sets the destructive item apart.
Destructive itemLast, after the separator. Red label and icon, red-tinted highlight. Items that open a confirmation end in an ellipsis.
Row tintThe row whose menu is open keeps a light tint (tr:has(:popover-open)), so the target stays clear while the menu covers its neighbors.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onClick or tap on ⋯, or Enter / Space on a focused ⋯. The button carries commandfor and command="toggle-popover", so the same press closes it again.
PlacementBelow the trigger, right edges aligned. Near the bottom of the viewport it flips above; near the left edge it aligns left edges instead. A popover opened by commandfor is anchored to that button implicitly, so there is no anchor-name to manage.
MotionFades in and slides 6px out of the trigger, 140ms, expo ease-out. When flipped above, it rises instead (@container anchored(fallback: flip-block)). Closes in 100ms. No motion with reduced-motion set.
FocusThe first item has autofocus, which a popover honors when it opens. After a keyboard open it shows the focus ring; after a click it stays quiet until an arrow key is pressed.
Choosing an itemEach item carries commandfor and command="hide-popover", so it closes the menu and focus returns to ⋯. In an app the item is also a link or a form button that carries the row’s id; the command only handles the closing.
One menu per rowEach row renders its own menu right after its trigger, so items can follow the row’s state. A single shared menu also works, and the opener is on the toggle event’s source, but it costs script: one more click to switch rows (the other row’s ⋯ counts as the same invoker and just closes it), and :has() can no longer tell which trigger is open.
DismissEsc, a click outside, the same ⋯ again, or choosing an item. Another row’s ⋯ closes this menu and opens its own in one click.

Code

Accessibility

KeyDoes
Enter Space on ⋯Open the menu with focus on the first item; press again to close.
↓ ↑Move between enabled items, wrapping at the ends (native focusgroup).
Home EndFirst and last item.
Enter Space on an itemRun it; the menu closes and focus returns to ⋯.
EscClose and return focus to ⋯.
TabMoves to the next control, but the menu stays open until the next click or Esc. See the gaps below.
  • focusgroup="menu" on the panel infers role="menu" and menuitem on each button; <hr> is exposed as a separator. The trigger states aria-haspopup="menu", and Chrome reports it as expanded while its popover is open, with nothing to keep in sync.
  • Every ⋯ has an aria-label naming its row (“Actions for INV-1048”). A column of identical “More” buttons is useless in a screen reader’s button list.
  • Hover and keyboard focus are separate: the pointer fills a row, the keyboard fills it and adds a ring. Merging them takes a pointermove listener that moves focus.
  • Gaps against the APG menu button, each a few lines of script if your product needs them: Tab doesn’t close the menu; after a pointer open the first ↓ lands on the second item, because focus already sits on the first; ↑ on the trigger doesn’t open at the last item; no typeahead.

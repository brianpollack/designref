Core libraries

The modern stack

Every concept here starts from the same four platform features. Together they replace what used to be a positioning library, a focus manager and a click-outside handler.

  • Popover puts the menu in the top layer, so no z-index or overflow: hidden ancestor can clip it, and gives light dismiss and Esc for free.
  • Anchor positioning places the menu against its trigger, or against a zero-size anchor at the pointer, and position-try-fallbacks flips it at the viewport edge.
  • Invoker commands (commandfor, command) open and close it from a button with no script.
  • focusgroup="menu" gives arrow-key navigation and infers the menu / menuitem roles natively.

What still needs script: reading the pointer position for a right-click, typeahead, hover-to-preview submenus, and anything that tracks pointer direction (safe triangles, radial picks).

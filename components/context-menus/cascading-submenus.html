Anatomy

Menu buttonOpens the root menu. Its own Tab stop, with a pressed state while its menu is open.
LevelEvery menu, root or nested, is the same surface: hairline border, two-layer shadow, 4px inner padding. Deeper levels stack above in the top layer.
ItemThree columns: a 16px icon slot, the label, and a shortcut, hint or chevron at the end. A level with no icons drops the slot.
Submenu triggerAn item with a chevron. It only opens its submenu and never runs a command; put a default action inside the submenu instead.
Chevron14px, hint color, hidden from screen readers. Points to the inline end even when the submenu flips, as on macOS and Windows.
Open pathEach trigger whose submenu is open keeps a tinted row, so the route from the root stays visible. Only the row under the pointer or keyboard focus is filled.
Safe triangleAn invisible area from where the pointer left a trigger to the near edge of its submenu. Turn on “Show safe area” to see it while moving.
HintSecondary text in the trailing column, such as “Current” on the folder the document is already in. Read by screen readers, unlike shortcuts.
Radio itemCheck mark in the icon slot; the group shares the submenu’s name.
Disabled and destructiveDisabled rows dim and are skipped by arrow keys. The destructive row is last, after a separator, red with a red fill on highlight.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onRoot menu: click, Enter, Space or ↓ on the menu button. Submenu: hover or keyboard focus on its trigger for 150ms, or →, Enter, Space or a click on it.
PlacementRoot menu 4px under its button, left edges aligned; near the right edge it aligns to the button’s right edge instead. Submenus sit beside their trigger with the first item level with it, overlapping the parent’s padding by 3px.
FlippingA submenu with no room on the inline end opens on the other side; near the bottom it grows upward from the trigger row. When neither side fits, as on a phone, it drops below the trigger, indented 16px so the trigger stays visible. Each level decides on its own.
Hover intentA submenu opens after the pointer or focus rests 150ms on its trigger, so passing over a row on the way elsewhere opens nothing. It closes 300ms after pointer and focus leave both trigger and submenu. Resting on a sibling trigger swaps submenus immediately.
Safe triangleStarts when the pointer leaves an open submenu’s trigger. Rows under it get no hover, so a diagonal path keeps the submenu open. It drops when the pointer rests for 300ms, turns back, reaches a submenu row, or the submenu closes. Mouse and trackpad only.
Focus and hoverHover moves focus without a ring, so the keyboard continues from the pointer. Keyboard focus on a trigger previews its submenu without entering it.
MotionEach level fades in and slides 4px away from its trigger: down for the root, sideways for a submenu, mirrored after a flip. 120ms in, 80ms out, ease-out. None with reduced motion.
CommandsA click or Enter runs the command, closes every level, and returns focus to the menu button. A radio holds for 250ms first so the moved check mark is seen.
DismissEsc or ← closes one level. Esc in the root menu, Tab, a click outside, or a command closes all of them.

Code

Accessibility

KeyDoes
↓ ↑Move between enabled items of one level, wrapping (native focusgroup). ↓ on the menu button opens the menu and enters it.
Home EndFirst and last enabled item of the level (native).
→On a submenu trigger: open the submenu and focus its first item.
Enter SpaceOn a submenu trigger: same as →. On a command: run it and close all levels. On a radio: check it, then close.
←In a submenu: close it and return focus to its trigger.
EscClose the current level and return focus to its trigger. In the root menu, close it and return to the menu button.
TabClose every level and move on. Focus is never trapped.
  • focusgroup="menu nomemory" sits on a div inside each popover, not on the popover. It infers role="menu" and menuitem, and each level is its own group, so arrow keys never leak into a submenu.
  • Triggers carry aria-haspopup="menu" and an aria-expanded that script keeps in sync on every toggle, including hover previews. Each menu is named by its trigger through aria-labelledby.
  • Each popover is the next sibling of its trigger, so the browser adds no implicit aria-details, and is nested inside its parent menu, so opening a level keeps its ancestors open.
  • Keyboard focus on a trigger shows the submenu as a preview; focus stays on the trigger until →, so screen reader users hear the trigger first, then can enter.
  • Chevrons and shortcuts are aria-hidden. Radio items set role="menuitemradio" and aria-checked inside a labelled role="group".
  • Not built here: typeahead. Add it when a level runs past about ten items.

Anatomy

SurfaceOne solid surface with 4px corners, 4px padding and the shadow16 elevation. 138px minimum width, 300px maximum, otherwise as wide as its longest item. A transparent 1px border shows up only in high contrast mode.
Item32px minimum height: a 20px line with 6px padding on every side. Corners match the surface at 4px. Items sit 2px apart.
SlotsCheckmark (16px), icon (20px), content, then secondary content, 4px apart. Content and secondary content add 2px of inline padding. Once any item has a checkmark or icon, every row reserves that slot so labels line up.
Secondary contentShortcut text in caption1 (12px) on the label’s 20px line, in Foreground3.
Group headerA 32px row in 12px semibold Foreground3 with 8px side padding. Labels a group of related items; here, the sort options.
Divider1px in Stroke2 with 4px above and below. It reaches past the surface padding to 1px from the edge.
Selectable itemsRadio and checkbox items show a 16px checkmark when checked and keep the empty slot when not. Fluent draws Checkmark16Filled.
Disabled itemLabel, icon and shortcut in ForegroundDisabled. No hover fill. Still focusable, so screen reader users hear that it exists.
Destructive itemFluent defines none. Use a plain item, a confirmation dialog, and, if the action needs emphasis, put it last after a divider.
Not built hereSubmenus (a 20px chevron in the last slot), split items, and the subText second line in caption2.

Specs

Behavior

Opens onA click on the trigger, Enter or Space, or ↓. commandfor and command="toggle-popover" handle the click with no script.
PlacementBelow the trigger with left edges aligned (Fluent’s below-start), no offset. Flips above when there is no room below.
MotionEnter only: a fade and a 10px slide away from the trigger over 400ms on decelerateMid. Closing is instant. No motion with reduced motion set.
StatesEach state is its own color token, not an overlay. Hover lightens the fill, darkens the label and turns the icon brand blue. Pressed uses the darker Pressed fill. Disabled items ignore both.
Hover and focusMoving the pointer over an item focuses it, so ↓ continues from the pointer. The outline appears only for keyboard focus.
SelectionA radio item checks and closes the menu. A checkbox item toggles and leaves the menu open.
DismissChoosing a command, Esc (focus returns to the trigger), a click outside, or Tab.

  • focusgroup="menu" infers role="menu" and menuitem. Sort options are menuitemradio in a group labelled by its header; the details pane is a menuitemcheckbox.
  • Disabled items use aria-disabled rather than disabled, matching Fluent: they stay in the arrow-key order and are announced as unavailable.
  • The trigger carries aria-haspopup="menu"; the browser reports aria-expanded from the popover state.
  • Focus outline: 2px colorStrokeFocus2, black on light (21:1) and white on dark (14.5:1).
  • Contrast: the Foreground2 label on the surface is 10:1 in both themes; Foreground3 shortcuts 6.2:1 light and 6.5:1 dark. Disabled text is intentionally below 3:1.
  • In forced colors, hover uses Highlight and HighlightText and disabled items use GrayText, as Fluent does.

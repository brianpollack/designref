Anatomy

Line tabsThe default. Each tab draws its own 2px underline in $border-subtle, so together they make one rule; tabs are 1px apart. Sits directly on the page background.
Contained tabsBlocks filled with $layer-accent and a 1px $border-strong divider. The selected block takes the $layer fill and a 2px top border, so it joins the panel below. Use when the tabs sit on a layer of their own.
Sizessm 32px, md 40px (line default), lg 48px (contained default). Tall contained tabs are 64px and can carry a secondary label.
Padding16px on each side (condensed density: 8px). Tabs share the row’s width in proportion to their labels; full-width contained tabs split it equally.
Labelbody-compact-01, 14 / 18 at weight 400; selected is heading-compact-01 at 600. Long labels truncate with an ellipsis.
Icon16px, 8px before the label, in the label’s color. A tab can also be icon-only: square, at the row’s height.
Also in CarbonDismissable tabs with a close button, a vertical layout, and overflow buttons that scroll a long row. Not built here.

Specs

Behavior

SelectingClick, or arrow to a tab: selection follows focus. Carbon’s activation prop switches to manual, where arrows move focus and Enter or Space selects.
HoverLine tabs: label turns $text-primary and the underline $border-strong. Contained tabs: the block fills with $layer-accent-hover. Selected tabs do not change.
SelectedLabel turns $text-primary at weight 600; the underline (line) or top border (contained) turns $border-interactive. There is no moving indicator: each tab draws its own.
MotionColors and borders ease over 70ms (duration-fast-01, productive standard).
DisabledThe label at 25% opacity, the underline stays $border-subtle, no hover. Arrow keys skip it.
Focus2px $focus outline inside the tab. Carbon styles :focus, so it also shows after a mouse click.

Anatomy

Primary tabsTop-level destinations, placed at the top of the content. 48dp tall with a label only, 64dp with an icon above the label. Tabs share the row equally.
Secondary tabsFilters or sub-views inside a primary tab’s content. 48dp, label only.
LabelTitleSmall: 14 / 20sp, Medium 500, 0.1px tracking. Selected primary labels are Primary; selected secondary labels are OnSurface; unselected are OnSurfaceVariant.
IconMaterial Symbols, 24dp, in the label’s color.
Primary indicator3dp tall, rounded, Primary. As wide as the tab’s content (the wider of icon and label), never under 24dp, sitting on the row’s bottom edge.
Secondary indicator3dp tall, square, Primary, spanning the whole tab.
Divider1dp SurfaceVariant under the row. The secondary token file defines it; the primary file has no divider entry, so this page reuses that value under primary tabs, as Compose’s tab row also draws one.
ScrollableFor rows that do not fit: tabs keep their own width (minimum 90dp) and the row starts 52dp in from the edge. Not shown here.

Specs

Behavior

SelectingClick, or arrow to a tab: focus and selection move together because panels are already on the page.
IndicatorSlides between tabs over 300ms on the emphasized easing. The primary indicator resizes to each tab’s content as it goes. Reduced motion jumps it.
StatesHover, focus and press all use one color change: an unselected tab’s label and icon go from OnSurfaceVariant to OnSurface. A selected primary tab stays Primary in every state.
RippleCompose also draws a ripple and state layer from the platform; this page leaves them out.
Focus3px Secondary ring drawn inside the tab, as for other Material controls on this site.
Two rowsA secondary row belongs to the primary tab above it. Each keeps its own selection; do not use both rows for the same level of choice.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabEnter a row at its selected tab; the next press goes to the panel.
← →Previous and next tab in the row, wrapping, selecting as it goes.
Home EndFirst and last tab in the row.
  • Each tab has role="tab", aria-selected and aria-controls; each panel has aria-labelledby. The two rows have their own aria-label.
  • Icons are decorative (aria-hidden): the label always names the tab.
  • Unselected label OnSurfaceVariant on Surface is 8.9:1 in the light scheme and 10.9:1 in dark, and the Primary label is 6.1:1 light and 10.9:1 dark.
  • Selected state is also shown by the indicator, not color alone.

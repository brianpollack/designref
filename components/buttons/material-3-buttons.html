Anatomy

TypesFilled (high emphasis), tonal and outlined (medium), elevated (high, with a shadow) and text (low). Filled is for the one final action of a flow, such as Save or Confirm; text is for dialogs and cards.
ContainerThe visible shape. Height by size: 32, 40, 56, 96, 136. Text and outlined buttons have no fill; outlined draws a 1px (2px large, 3px extra large) OutlineVariant line.
LabelLabelLarge 14/20 Medium for extra small and small, TitleMedium 16/24 for medium, HeadlineSmall 24/32 for large, HeadlineLarge 32/40 for extra large. Padding each side: 12, 16, 24, 48, 64.
IconOptional, before the label: 20, 20, 24, 32, 40, with a gap of 4, 8, 8, 12, 16. The padding does not change when an icon is present.
ShapeRound (a pill) by default, or square (12, 12, 16, 28, 28). A toggle in its selected state takes the other shape: round becomes square, square becomes round (small stays 12).
State layerThe label color over the container at 0.08 hover, 0.10 focus, 0.10 pressed.
Touch target48 × 48 minimum. Here the button element is the 48px target and is transparent; the container sits inside it.
Connected groupItems 40 tall, 2px apart. Outer ends are fully round, inner corners 8px, 4px while pressed; the selected item is fully round.
Dialog actionsText buttons at the bottom right of the dialog, 8px apart, confirm on the right.

Specs

Every size, shape, opacity and color role is a Compose token read from the files cited below; colors are the baseline scheme resolved to hex. These values are ours, because no token or number was found:

  • Focus ring: Secondary is the token (FocusIndicatorColor); the 3px width and drawing it outside the container are our choice.
  • Disabled label color differs between files: the filled, tonal, outlined, elevated and text files say OnSurfaceVariant, the baseline file says OnSurface. This page uses OnSurfaceVariant for the label and OnSurface for the icon, both at 0.38. The outlined disabled outline is OutlineVariant at full strength, as the token reads.
  • Shadow offsets and blur for each elevation level (the levels 0, 1, 3, 6 dp are tokens). Compose draws a platform shadow.
  • Spring timing: the effects spring (damping 1.0, stiffness 1600) is read from the motion tokens; its 170ms settle time and the sampled easing curve are computed here.
  • Text button padding: Compose’s legacy TextButtonContentPadding is 12dp and its source recommends the shared padding instead; this page uses the size tokens for every type.
  • The group’s colors (filled toggle) are our pick; the group tokens define only shape, gap and height.

Behavior

PressThe corners morph from the resting shape to the pressed shape (8, 8, 12, 16, 16) and back, using a critically damped spring: no overshoot, no bounce. Compose animates only between rounded shapes.
ToggleClick flips selection. The container changes color and shape together: filled goes SurfaceContainer to Primary, tonal to Secondary, elevated to Primary, outlined to InverseSurface. Text buttons have no toggle.
ElevationFilled and tonal sit at 0 and lift to 1dp on hover. Elevated rests at 1dp and lifts to 3dp. Pressed returns to rest.
DisabledContainer OnSurface at 0.10, label and icon at 0.38, no shadow, no state layer, not focusable.
Connected groupPick one: the chosen item goes fully round and filled; pressed neighbors tighten their inner corners to 4px.
Dialog actionsConfirm is first in the source and rightmost on screen. When the two do not fit in the dialog’s width (minimum 280) they wrap: confirm on top, dismiss below, both right-aligned, 8px apart.
Reduced motionShape and layer transitions are removed; state still changes.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabNext button; disabled buttons are skipped. The connected group is one stop.
Enter SpaceActivate; the press shape shows while the key is held.
← →Move the choice within the connected group (native radios).
  • Real <button> elements; toggles carry aria-pressed; the group is a radiogroup of native radios with a label.
  • Icons are decorative; the label always names the action. An icon-only button would need an aria-label.
  • Selected state is shown by shape as well as color.
  • Confirm precedes dismiss in tab order but sits to its right on screen, as Compose does. Screen reader order is confirm, then dismiss.
  • Rules check: WCAG exempts disabled controls from contrast, but the automatic check may still measure their labels; a contrast item listed for a disabled label is that, not a defect.

Anatomy

ButtonA button with a label left-aligned in the container and an optional 16px icon at the inline end. 1px border, square corners, 14px label at weight 400. Padding is 16px at the start and 64px at the end, so the label has room for the icon.
PrimaryBlue fill, white label. The principal action; one per screen.
SecondaryGray fill (Gray 80 on White, Gray 60 on Gray 100). Pairs with a primary as its “negative” action: Cancel, Back.
TertiaryTransparent with a 1px blue (white on Gray 100) border and label; fills on hover. Can stand alone.
GhostNo fill or border; a blue label. Padding is 16px each side and the icon sits 8px after the label. The least prominent kind.
DangerThree styles: primary (red fill), tertiary (red border) and ghost (red label). For destructive actions.
Sizesxs 24, sm 32, md 40, lg 48 (default), xl 64, 2xl 80px. Expressive lg (16px label, 20px icon) is also 48px and is not built. xl and 2xl are for buttons that bleed to the edge of a modal, side panel or tearsheet. Do not mix sizes in a group.
Icon onlyA square at the size’s height with the icon centered. It has no label, so it needs an aria-label; Carbon wraps it in a tooltip, which is not built here.
Button setFixed groups sit 16px apart at equal widths. Fluid sets (modal footers) are flush with a 1px $button-separator and take 50% each (two buttons) or 25% each (three or more), right-aligned.
Ours, not Carbon’sThe 16px fixed-group gap and equal-width grid are design guidance, not built into the component. The page chrome, modal card and panel text are demo surface. The reduced-motion rule is ours. Body-compact-01’s 18px line height is taken from the Carbon Tabs page, not from a type file read here, so label heights are unverified. Selected icon-only buttons, expressive sizes, skeleton, loading and the stacked fluid set are not built.

Specs

Behavior

Primary countOne primary button per screen, not counting the application header, a modal dialog or a side panel. A temporary flow that opens its own primary (a side panel over a data table) may show two. Not every page needs one: use tertiary and ghost buttons when the page is mostly content.
PlacementOn full-page designs the primary is on the left of the group and the group is left-aligned. In wizards and dialogs it is on the right. Carbon notes this guidance changed from keeping the primary on the right in a left-aligned group. In a stacked group the primary is on top, 16px above the rest.
Modal footersTwo buttons: secondary left, primary right, 50% each and full bleed. Three: 25% each, right-aligned, only the rightmost may be primary; if all three weigh the same, make them all secondary. Single button: right, 50%. Danger modal: the primary becomes a danger primary. Progress: Cancel is ghost at the left; Previous (secondary) and Next (primary) share the right half; the last step’s Next takes the final action’s name.
HoverFilled kinds go to their Hover token. Tertiary fills and its label turns $text-inverse. Ghost fills with $background-hover and the label turns $link-primary-hover. Danger tertiary and ghost fill red and turn white. Hover rules apply only on devices that hover.
PressFilled kinds go to their Active token; ghost to $background-active. Tertiary loses its border.
FocusCarbon styles :focus: the border and a 1px inset ring in $focus, then a 2px inner line in $background. Ghost icon-only buttons use the 1px ring only. Tertiary fills with its own color on focus.
DisabledFilled kinds: gray fill and border, label $text-on-color-disabled. Tertiary and ghost: transparent, label $text-disabled. cursor: not-allowed, no hover or ring.
MotionFill, shadow, border and outline ease over 70ms (duration-fast-01) on the productive entrance curve.
LabelsVerb plus noun, sentence case, left-aligned; common actions (Done, Close, Cancel, Add, Delete) may stand alone. Never a noun alone. Long labels wrap to a second line instead of truncating. Modal buttons name the task (Add, Delete, Save); avoid Done or OK.

Avoid

Don’tWhy
Two high-emphasis buttons in a groupThe hierarchy is lost; a layout should have a single high-emphasis button.
Secondary with a non-primary buttonSecondary is only the partner of a primary, and never used alone or for a positive action.
Tertiary with danger tertiaryTwo outlined buttons that differ only by color.
Primary with danger ghostListed under combinations to avoid; use danger tertiary or a danger primary pair instead.
Tertiary with danger ghostListed under combinations to avoid.
Different sizes or widths in one groupGroups read as one object; the longest label sets every width, and sizes are not mixed.
More than three buttonsToo many calls to action overwhelm. Group related actions in a menu button, or use a toolbar.
A tertiary button in a fluid setFluid sets use primary, secondary and (in a modal) ghost.
A button that navigatesUse a link when the action takes the user to a new page.
Truncated or centered labelsWrap long labels; keep them left-aligned even in a wide button.

Code

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabMoves between buttons in DOM order, which is also the visual order: primary first in a left-aligned group, last in a wizard or dialog.
Enter SpaceActivates the focused button.
  • In a dialog with no form field the primary button takes initial focus; on a form, Enter activates the primary when focus is on a control that does not use it.
  • Icon-only buttons carry an aria-label; icons next to a label are decorative. A group has role="group" and a name.
  • The 1px separator between fluid buttons gives the 3:1 distinction between adjacent controls that Carbon recommends.
  • xs (24px) and the icon-only xs button meet the WCAG 2.2 24px minimum, not the 44px recommendation; sm (32px) is also below it. The rules table below measures size, contrast, names, focus style and reduced motion. It cannot judge how well the focus ring reads against every neighbor, or label wording.

Anatomy

Action partThe label. Clicking it runs the default action immediately. It is a normal button with a verb, and it should not appear again in the menu (Fluent).
Menu partA square 40px button with a chevron that opens the alternatives. NN/g: it needs a visible divider, and both parts should be at least about 1cm for touch.
DividerDivided style: a 1px line in the label color at 35%. Connected style: a 2px gap and tight inner corners (4px) instead.
MenuA popover under the whole control: three to a dozen items, no submenus (NN/g). Items are plain verbs; the default action is not among them.

Specs

Heights and the 2px gap and 4px inner corner follow Material 3’s small split button (40px, 2px, 4px; the inner corner opens to 12px on hover). Colors and the divided style are ours.

Behavior

When to useOnly when one action is clearly the default most of the time and the rest are variations of it (Save and Save as; Reply and Reply all). NN/g: with no strong default, use a menu button.
OpenThe menu part opens a popover through popovertarget: light dismiss, Esc to close and top-layer rendering come with it. Items close it with commandfor and command="hide-popover", so closing needs no script.
PositionAnchored below the invoking button and aligned to its start; position-try-fallbacks: flip-block flips it above at the viewport edge.
KeysEnter or Space on the menu part opens the menu; focusgroup="menu nomemory" gives ↑ ↓ with wrap. APG’s menu button also allows ↓ to open and move to the first item; this demo opens on activation only.
NamesThe menu part is named for what it opens (“More save options”). Where menu items drop a word from the main label (Fluent’s “New” and “Event”), carry the full phrase with aria-describedby.
DisabledDisable both parts together, or only the menu part if there are no alternatives right now.
Reduced motionThe corner morph and fill transitions stop.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabAction part, then menu part.
Enter SpaceRun the action, or open the menu.
↑ ↓Move through the open menu.
EscClose the menu and return focus to the menu part.
  • Two buttons in a labelled group, not one control with two halves: each has its own name, role and focus.
  • The menu part has aria-haspopup="menu" and the popover relationship sets aria-expanded implicitly.
  • The status line is a polite live region so a screen-reader user hears what ran.
  • Focus ring is 2px with a 2px offset and lifts above its neighbor so the shared edge is never hidden.

How many, and which

QuestionWhat the systems say
How many loud buttons?One. Apple: one or two prominent buttons per view. Fluent: one primary per layout. Carbon: one primary per page, and a modal’s one primary. Material: the more important the action, the higher its emphasis.
Several equal actions?Make them all quiet. Fluent: with more than two equal buttons use neutral backgrounds. Carbon: with more than three, tertiary or ghost, or a menu button or toolbar.
Where does the main action go?They disagree: last (Apple, Material, Carbon dialogs) or first (Fluent, Windows). Pick one for the product and keep it. Action Pairs shows both.
How many in a dialog?Up to three (Apple, Fluent). Carbon fixes layouts for one, two and three.
Which labels?A verb for the outcome: “Delete file”, not “OK”. Fluent and Carbon use sentence case; Apple asks for title-style capitalization.
How big?At least 24px (WCAG 2.2 minimum); 44px for touch (Apple, WCAG enhanced); 48dp for Material.

Core libraries

The modern stack

A button needs no library. These platform features replace what used to be scripts and extra markup.

  • Nesting and custom properties let one component set three private colors per variant, so every state rule reads the same properties and never repeats a hex.
  • color-mix() derives hover and pressed from the base color, in both themes, with light-dark() choosing whether to mix toward black or white.
  • :has() styles a segment from its own checked input, so segmented controls and toggles need no script, and groups of bubbles or buttons join up from their siblings.
  • Container queries make a button row stack, wrap or fold into icons by the space it has, not the window’s.
  • Popover, commandfor and anchor positioning open a split button’s menu, close it, and place it, with no script.
  • interestfor shows a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus.
  • @property, linear(), corner-shape and backdrop-filter make the expressive effects possible in CSS alone.
  • reading-flow keeps keyboard order equal to visual order when a row is reversed for a platform convention.

What still needs script: a toggle button’s aria-pressed, the roving keys of a toolbar, blocking a click on an aria-disabled button, timers for loading and hold-to-confirm, and anything that talks to the network. Every page also carries a Rules check that measures target size, contrast, names, focus and reduced motion in both themes.

Anatomy

GroupA fieldset with a (visually hidden) legend naming the choice. For radios it is the radio group; for checkboxes, the set.
SegmentA label around a native input. 40px high, equal padding, the same label weight selected or not. The input fills the label, hidden but focusable, so the whole segment is the hit area.
SelectedFilled and, in the connected styles, a check before the label (Material 3 shows a check on the selected segment). A thumb in the track style. Never color alone.
ConnectedOne outline, shared dividers, pill ends. Material’s segmented button is 40px high with a 1px outline and 12px side padding.
TrackA trough with one thumb that slides to the chosen segment: a single pseudo-element positioned by anchor positioning, as in the underline tabs.
Toggle buttonOne button whose pressed state is on or off, with a label that never changes. Fluent: toggle buttons suit toolbars; in a settings panel use a switch.

Which control

Choose exactly one of two to fiveSegmented control (radios). Apple: about five to seven segments on a wide screen and no more than about five on iPhone; equal widths; text or images, not both mixed; nouns or noun phrases.
Choose exactly one of manyA pop-up or select. Apple: radio groups are typically two to five, and a pop-up above that.
Switch several on or offToggle buttons in a group, as filters.
One setting, on or offA switch in a list or panel; a toggle button in a toolbar. Apple: the iOS switch appears only in a list row.
Switch between views of the same contentTabs or a segmented control. If the change loads a different page, use links, not radios.

Specs

Our own values, near Material 3’s segmented button (40px, 1px outline, pill, check on selected). Apple’s and Fluent’s pages give no numbers for these, so none are quoted from them.

Behavior

RadiosArrow keys move and select together, wrap at the ends, and Tab enters the group at the checked one: the browser does all of this for a native radio group. APG’s radio group describes the same.
CheckboxesEach is its own tab stop; Space toggles. They read as “checkbox, checked”. If you want “toggle button, pressed”, use buttons with aria-pressed, as the single toggle here does; choose by the meaning you want announced.
Toggle labelDoes not change with state (APG): “Pin to top” stays, and aria-pressed carries on or off. If the label changes instead (“Pin” and “Unpin”), drop aria-pressed.
State is not colorCheck mark, fill, and thumb position all mark selection. Apple: make the visual difference between toggle states obvious and do not rely on color alone.
DisabledA disabled segment stays in the group, dimmed, and is skipped by arrow keys. Say why nearby if it matters.
Selection moves instantlyThe segmented control changes the view at once; if choosing triggers slow work, show loading on the content, not on the control.
Reduced motionThe thumb jumps instead of sliding.
Forced colorsSelected segments use Highlight and HighlightText, and the thumb becomes a Highlight outline.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabInto a radio group at the checked segment; out to the next control.
← → ↑ ↓Previous or next radio, selecting it; wrap at the ends.
SpaceCheck the focused radio; toggle the focused checkbox or toggle button.
  • The selected segment’s label is 10.7:1 (light) and 9.4:1 (dark) on its fill. The connected outline is 2.2:1 on white, short of the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 asks for a component boundary; selection is therefore also carried by fill and a check, and the label contrast carries identification.
  • Each segment is 40px high and at least about 64px wide, over WCAG 2.2’s 24px minimum.
  • The legend names the choice for a screen reader; there is no visible heading because the buttons say it themselves.

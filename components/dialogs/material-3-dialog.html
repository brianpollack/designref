Anatomy

IconOptional, 24dp, in the secondary role, above the headline.
HeadlineOne line for the question or the decision. Headline role, the on-surface color.
Supporting textWhat happens next. Body role, the on-surface-variant color.
ActionsUp to two text buttons, the dismissive one first and the confirming one last, on the trailing end.
ContainerSurface container high, elevation level 3, 28dp corners, 24dp padding.

Specs

The container, padding, width, spacing and corner, and the color roles, are taken from Compose’s dialog tokens. The scrim and shadow are ours: the page gives the level 3 elevation, not the shadow values.

Behavior

When to useAlerts, simple confirmations and quick choices, with no more than two actions here. For several actions or a choice of many items, use a different pattern.
WidthBetween 280dp and 560dp: the container grows with its content until it reaches the maximum, then wraps.
ActionsThe confirming action sits on the trailing end, with 8dp between buttons; when they wrap, the confirming action goes on top.
DismissalEscape, the scrim, or an action. A confirmation that loses data can be locked so only an action closes it: see Destructive Confirm.
MotionEnter and exit fade and rise 8px over 200ms. Reduced motion removes it.

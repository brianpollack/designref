Anatomy

TitleA brief verb phrase, sentence case. Named by aria-labelledby.
CloseA 48px icon button top right with an accessible name.
ContentBody text and any form. Carbon’s padding is 8px above, 16px at the sides and 48px below, so the footer never touches the text.
Footer64px tall. Two buttons at 50% each, or three at 25%. Only the outermost right button may be primary.

Specs

Widths (60% at the lg size, 42% for the small variant), padding, the 64px footer and the 50% and 25% button flex values come from Carbon’s modal stylesheet. Button height, colors and the overlay are ours.

Behavior

SizesFour responsive sizes: extra small, small, medium and large. Carbon: brief text suits extra small or small; complex content, such as a data table, the default or large size.
Two buttonsSecondary on the left, primary on the right, each spanning 50%.
Three buttonsEach 25%, aligned to the right. Only the outermost right button is primary.
One buttonOn the right side, spanning 50% of the modal.
DangerA transactional modal for a destructive or irreversible action. The primary button becomes a danger button, and the title should say what is lost.
LabelsVerbs for what happens. Avoid “Done” and “OK”.

Accessibility

KeyDoes
TabMoves between the close button, the footer buttons and back again.
EscCloses without a value.
EnterActivates the focused button.
  • The focus ring is drawn inside the button (negative offset) so it is never clipped by the footer edge.
  • Forced-colors mode keeps the border on the modal and the focus outline in the system highlight color.

Anatomy

TitleNames the object and the action: “Delete ‘Design Research’?”, not “Are you sure?”.
ConsequenceWhat is lost, how much, and who is affected, in one or two sentences. This is the part that makes the confirm worth its interruption.
ActionsCancel on the left, quiet and first to receive focus. The destructive action on the right, in the danger color, labelled with its verb and object.

Specs

The 420px width and padding are ours. The danger color is ours, chosen to read as destructive at 4.5:1 in both themes; the pattern follows the sources below.

Behavior

When to useOnly for destructive or irreversible actions. Carbon: the danger modal for these. Fluent: alert dialogs for potential loss. Don’t put a confirm on every action.
Dismissalclosedby="none" makes Escape and the outside click do nothing. A close button would give a third way out, so this page has none. Fluent: alert dialogs can only be dismissed by a footer button.
Initial focusThe APG says that for a destructive final step, focus may start on the least destructive action. Here it is Cancel, so Enter does not delete by accident.
ReturnAfter Cancel or Delete, focus returns to the Delete workspace button that opened it.

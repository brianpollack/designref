Anatomy

SheetOn a phone, a full-width panel anchored to the bottom edge with 16px top corners.
Grab handleA 36 × 4px bar at the top. It shows the sheet can be dismissed by a drag on a phone; hidden on wide screens.
ModalFrom 640px up, the same dialog is a 440px centred panel with all four corners rounded.
FooterTwo buttons sharing the width on a phone and at the end on wide screens.

Specs

These values are ours: a neutral default, not a system’s. The 640px break is a common tablet width, not a published one.

Behavior

Why one elementA dialog that changes shape by width keeps one focus order, one set of names and one close path. A second element for the phone would have to stay in sync.
Open and closeOpened with showModal(), so it is modal at every width. Escape, a backdrop click and the footer buttons all close it.
MotionOn a phone the sheet slides up 100%; on a wide screen it rises 12px and fades. Both over 240ms. Reduced motion removes the movement.
Drag to dismissNot included: it needs script. The grab handle is decorative here; the close paths above are the real ones.
